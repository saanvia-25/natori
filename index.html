Welcome one and all to the second edition of my beautiful Natori fanpage!!! If you're new here, this is a website-- the second one now-- dedicated to my favorite music artist Natori (stylized as "natori"). He often draws on multiple genres, using themes from vocaloid, city pop, R&B, and Western pop music, and is well known for his smooth and expressive singing style. Basically, I could listen to him all day and night and still feel like I haven't listened to enough of him!

Since creating my first fanpage, I have evolved as an enjoyer of music, and so has Natori! He's now created opening and ending themes for THREE different anime series, with his latest one being the ED for the hit anime Oshi no Ko. He's created so many more songs, and has even released his SECOND album earlier this year, named The Abyss. Yes, you heard me right. His second album!!! I feel like a proud parent *sniff*. And so, what better way to celebrate his progress by creating a second edition of the Natori fanpage?
